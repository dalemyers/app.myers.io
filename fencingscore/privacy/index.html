Fencing Score Privacy Policy

How Fencing Score handles your information.

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Fencing Score ("the app") is a simple fencing scoreboard and bout timer for iOS. Your privacy is straightforward: the app works entirely on your device and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Fencing Score has no account, no sign-in, and no server. Scores and the bout timer exist only while you use the app and are not tied to your identity.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your preferences — such as whether sound and vibration are enabled — locally on your device. This information stays on the device, is not sent anywhere, and is removed if you delete the app. For troubleshooting, the app keeps short-lived diagnostic logs on the device only; these are not transmitted to us or anyone else.

No network use

Fencing Score does not connect to the internet to run. It does not send analytics, does not use advertising networks, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Fencing Score is free and contains no in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

Fencing Score is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information, none is collected from children either.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email support@fencingscore.myers.io.
