Frequently asked questions

How are match-ups generated?

In Normal mode, Pickled uses constraint solving to create balanced pairings each round, tracking who has played with and against whom so it can minimize repeat match-ups and keep sit-outs fair. King of the Court mode instead moves winners up a court and losers down between rounds.

Is my data synced across devices?

Yes. Your players and locations are synced across your devices through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID and that iCloud is enabled for Pickled in Settings.

How many players do I need to start a session?

A minimum of four players for doubles, or two for singles.

Is session history saved?

Players and locations are saved permanently. Session results are shown while a session is active; saved session history is on our list of future improvements.

What are the system requirements?

Pickled runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18.5 or later. Live Activities and widgets require a compatible device.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?

Email us at support@pickled.myers.io with your idea or the problem you ran into. We read every message.
