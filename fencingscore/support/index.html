Frequently asked questions

How do I keep score?

Each fencer has their own score with plus and minus buttons. Tap plus to add a touch and minus to correct a mistake. The scores stay large and readable from a distance.

How does the bout timer work?

The timer starts at three minutes. Tap Start to run it and Reset to set it back. You can nudge the time up or down with the +1 / -1 controls.

Can I swap the fencers' sides?

Yes. Use the swap control between the two scores to switch sides when the fencers change ends.

Can I turn the sounds or vibration off?

Yes. Open Settings from the gear icon to toggle sound and vibration feedback.

Why doesn't my screen dim during a bout?

That's intentional — Fencing Score keeps the display awake so the score is always visible while you referee.

What are the system requirements?

Fencing Score runs on iPhone with iOS 16.6 or later. It's free.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?

Email us at support@fencingscore.myers.io with your idea or the problem you ran into. We read every message.
